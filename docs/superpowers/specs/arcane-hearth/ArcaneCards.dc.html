Arcane graph cards

Each card takes any Home Assistant sensor. Animations stop when the phone asks for reduced motion.

arcane-orb-card
Any percent: devices online, battery, washer progress. Colours: mana, health, glow.
arcane-trail-card
Draws a glowing spell trail from sensor history; tap for 24 h, 7 d, 30 d.
arcane-rune-gauge
Thermostats, humidity, disk space. Rings turn slowly; the arc fills to the value.
arcane-flow-card
Mana flow for a power sensor: the more watts, the faster the sparks circle. Needs a power or energy sensor.
arcane-bars-card
Compare rooms or days: lights on, minutes of music, quests done.
arcane-stamina-card
Thermostat Büro
Climate sensor
Every battery as a stamina bar; empty ones glow softly and sort to the top.
arcane-level-card
Level 4 Hearthkeeper70 XP to go
Your Cuest hero on the dashboard: level, XP, streak flame.
arcane-quest-card Change the Büro battery +30 XP
A fault becomes a Cuest quest; finishing it pays XP.
